# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to be respectful, evidence-oriented and constructive. Harassment,
discrimination, credential leakage and knowingly fabricated scientific claims are not acceptable.

Disagreement about a result is welcome and is settled by evidence: an artifact, a reproduction or a
failure to reproduce.

**Reporting.** Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer, **arjhine@experimentalmachines.org**.
Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions that breach this
code, and may block a contributor who repeatedly does.
