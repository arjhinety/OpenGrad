# Studies

OpenGrad's research is published as numbered studies. Each study has a permanent landing page at
`https://opengrad.arjhinety.com/studies/<id>`, and the site's `/` shows the latest frozen study.

## Versioning rules

- **Freezing.** A study is frozen when its findings are final and an independent claim audit of the
  repository, the Hugging Face cards and the site has every finding resolved and logged under
  [`reports/audits/`](../../reports/audits/study-001-claim-audit/README.md). The freeze is an
  annotated git tag (`study-001`, `study-002`, …) in this repository, a tag of the same name in
  `opengrad-site` on the commit that froze its page, and a tag of the same name on each of the
  study's Hugging Face repositories. The study's page links its evidence at the tag, not at
  `master`, so a reader lands on the evidence the page was written from.
- **After a freeze, the findings do not change silently.** A number later found wrong in a frozen
  study is corrected in [`reports/ERRATA.md`](../../reports/ERRATA.md), the same way frozen,
  hash-pinned reports are corrected. The study's checkpoints, corpora and evaluations are not
  modified, relabelled or replaced. Navigation, links to the study's own artifacts, and sections
  that present evidence already committed at the study's tag may still be added to its page,
  generated from that tag; none of them may change a number. Study 001's quantization section
  (2026-09-13) is one.
- **New work goes into the next study.** Any experiment started after a freeze belongs to the next
  study. It is pre-registered before it runs, and it is reported against the frozen study's
  checkpoints, not in place of them. Its models and datasets go to new Hugging Face repositories,
  so the frozen study's links keep pointing at what they describe.
- **Guardrails.** Every study from 002 on follows [GUARDRAILS.md](GUARDRAILS.md): rules derived
  from the 100 claims Study 001 got wrong, with a checklist before GPU time is spent and one
  before anything is published or frozen.

## Study 001 — tool-policy post-training on Qwen3.5-2B

**Status: FROZEN 2026-09-13** at tag [`study-001`](https://github.com/arjhinety/OpenGrad/tree/study-001).
Landing page: [opengrad.arjhinety.com/studies/001](https://opengrad.arjhinety.com/studies/001).

It covers the B0 baseline, the five SFT arms, both DPO lineages, the H200 general-capability
diagnosis, the GGUF PTQ ladder and the OpenWeights ParitySuite run. On the confirmatory partition
(n=1,277), call F1 rose from 0.6264 (B0) to 0.7470 with SFT (M0) and 0.7548 after DPO (M1-v2,
+0.0078 over M0, within noise). M1-v2 was promoted under the parent-relative `tool_use_promotion_v4`
gate, introduced after M0 was evaluated. It refuses all 1,319 zero-shot GSM8K questions, a
general-capability regression associated with tool-policy post-training on When2Call-derived data
that the promotion gate could not see. Causation is not established.

- Full intervention record: [`docs/EXPERIMENT_RESULTS.md`](../EXPERIMENT_RESULTS.md)
- Diagnosis: [`reports/GENERAL_CAPABILITY_REGRESSION.md`](../../reports/GENERAL_CAPABILITY_REGRESSION.md)
  and [`reports/FINAL_CAMPAIGN_AUDIT.md`](../../reports/FINAL_CAMPAIGN_AUDIT.md)
- Corrections: [`reports/ERRATA.md`](../../reports/ERRATA.md) and the
  [claim audit](https://opengrad.arjhinety.com/studies/001/claim-audit.pdf), 93 findings with their
  resolutions, plus 7 from the pre-freeze site recheck; the full ledger is in
  [`reports/audits/study-001-claim-audit/`](../../reports/audits/study-001-claim-audit/README.md)
- Models and datasets: the
  [OpenGrad Study 001 collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/arjhinety/opengrad-study-001)
  on Hugging Face, in reading order with a note per item. Each repository carries a `study-001`
  tag at its frozen revision
- Quantized formats: the promoted checkpoint as BF16 GGUF and nine llama.cpp quantizations in

## Study 002 — the refusal/direct-answer mechanism (refusal relabelling)

**Status: REOPENED (2026-09-25): second `P-UNANS` attempt, drawn, not yet labelled.** The first attempt stopped before training: preregistered stop rule 2 fired when the unanswerable set `P-UNANS` could not reach its labeller-agreement floor (raw agreement 0.770 against 0.80; [negative result](../../reports/study-002/punans-v1/NEGATIVE-RESULT.md)). The owner first accepted that stop as final, then reopened the study the same day under [44](study-002/44-PUNANS-V2-AMENDMENT-DRAFT.md) (`study_002_prereg_v12`); its trial and main sets are drawn and unlabelled. No GPU time has been spent, and no model result exists. Landing page:
[opengrad.arjhinety.com/studies/002](https://opengrad.arjhinety.com/studies/002). Design set:
[`docs/research/study-002/`](study-002/README.md).

**Scope — revised, and the revision is deliberate.** This study was originally scoped as three subjects
(relabelling, on-policy distillation, speculative decoding). It is now **one**: the mechanism of the
refusal/direct-answer regression, its boundary conditions, and one corrective intervention. The other two
subjects become [Study 003](study-002/18-STUDY-003-ROADMAP.md) and
[Study 004](study-002/19-STUDY-004-ROADMAP.md), because two of the three have no live implementation — so
pre-registering thresholds for them now would be a decision rule written before the thing it decides about
exists — and because running three unrelated causal claims inside one freeze is how Study 001 accumulated
100 unsupported claims. The reasoning is recorded in
[`study-002/README.md`](study-002/README.md) rather than made silently.

Scope, pre-registered before any GPU time is spent, against the
[guardrails](GUARDRAILS.md) checklist:

1. **Dataset corrections, starting with refusal relabelling** ([`ROADMAP.md`](../../ROADMAP.md)
   step 16, `BLOCKED_ON_PREFLIGHT`). A heuristic detector flags 18,114 of Canonical-v2's 173,237
   records as refusal-shaped targets labelled ANSWER. The detector's precision is measured on a
   hand-labelled sample first. Relabelling goes into a new corpus version; Canonical-v2 stays as
   published. M0 is retrained with only the labels changed, and GSM8K zero-shot, IFEval, MMLU-Pro
   and the tool-policy metrics are re-measured against pre-registered thresholds. A null result is
   publishable.
2. **On-policy distillation (M2, RQ4) — deferred to [Study 003](study-002/18-STUDY-003-ROADMAP.md).**
   Study 001 did not run it: the trainer seams exist, but the live training path is unimplemented and the
   one recorded M2 attempt is mock-only ([`reports/M2_DECISION.md`](../../reports/M2_DECISION.md)). It
   needs a live path, a teacher that fits the available hardware, and its own confirmatory evaluation, so
   it gets its own pre-registration rather than sharing this one.
3. **Speculative decoding integration (RQ5, ROADMAP step 14) — deferred to
   [Study 004](study-002/19-STUDY-004-ROADMAP.md).** Target-attached or native speculative decoding for
   decode efficiency without a separate draft model. The benchmark suite has a speculative tier; no
   runtime support exists and nothing has been measured
   (`BLOCKED_MISSING_MTP_COMPONENT`).

Cross-model replication (step 11) and joint capability-efficiency studies (step 15) stay on the
roadmap and are not assigned to Study 002.
## Study 002 — what the design set contains

**Design set:** [`docs/research/study-002/`](study-002/README.md) — forty numbered outputs (listed in its README), from the adversarial
audit of Study 001 to the closure report. The parts a reader is most likely to want:

- **Why the regression happened and why nobody saw it.** Study 001's promotion gate asserted a floor on
  `no_call_accuracy`, a dimension whose truth class is `ANSWER`, on a confirmatory partition whose gold
  labels are `CALL` 453, `UNSUPPORTED` 453, `CLARIFY` 371 and **`ANSWER` 0**. The metric was computed over
  an empty denominator and returned `0.0` for every model including the baseline, so the floor rejected
  every candidate unconditionally and had to be relaxed. The population has never been able to represent
  "answering directly was the right thing to do"
  ([`06-SPLIT-SPEC.md`](study-002/06-SPLIT-SPEC.md), [`07-METRIC-SPEC.md`](study-002/07-METRIC-SPEC.md)).
- **A four-mode confirmatory partition** covering `ANSWER`, `CALL`, `CLARIFY` and `UNSUPPORTED`, with a
  resolvability rule: every row prints its `n` and the margin that population could have resolved, and a
  narrower comparison is reported `WITHIN_NOISE` rather than as a finding.
- **`tool_use_promotion_v5`**, which keeps every v3/v4 threshold and adds the checks that would have caught
  this: mode coverage, a minimum `answer_rate` and maximum `refusal_rate` on answerable items, a
  `refusal_correctness` floor on genuinely unanswerable ones, and `NOT_EVALUABLE` as a verdict, because a
  gate that could not run must not be indistinguishable from one that ran and passed
  ([`11-THRESHOLDS.md`](study-002/11-THRESHOLDS.md)).
- **Eleven arms in three tiers**, each varying one factor, with the competing explanations run regardless
  of the primary result and a dose ladder on the same corpus
  ([`04-ARM-MATRIX.md`](study-002/04-ARM-MATRIX.md)).
- **A pre-GPU gate of fourteen blocking checks**, including a self-test that the new gate can actually fail,
  because a gate never observed failing has an untested failure path
  ([`16-GPU-READINESS-GATE.md`](study-002/16-GPU-READINESS-GATE.md)).

## Study 003 — on-policy distillation (M2, RQ4)

**Status: PLANNED. No pre-registration yet.** Roadmap:
[`study-002/18-STUDY-003-ROADMAP.md`](study-002/18-STUDY-003-ROADMAP.md).

Blocked on implementation, not on hardware: `reports/M2_DECISION.md` records that the trainer defaults to
mock rollout and mock teacher providers, that its live path raises `NotImplementedError`, that accepted rows
never update student weights, and that the existing M2 run has no valid checkpoints or evaluation artifacts.
The same document already specifies what a justifiable design must supply — a real pinned teacher, 
materialized prompt states excluding frozen evaluation IDs, on-policy rollouts, a defined teacher-guided
loss, and the same DEV/confirmatory discipline — and that list is Study 003's requirement set. It is
sequenced after Study 002 so that it can inherit the four-mode partition: a distillation study evaluated on a
three-mode set could not see the behaviour it might inherit.

## Study 004 — speculative decoding (RQ5, ROADMAP step 14)

**Status: PLANNED. No pre-registration yet. No runtime support exists.** Roadmap:
[`study-002/19-STUDY-004-ROADMAP.md`](study-002/19-STUDY-004-ROADMAP.md). Current measured status:
`BLOCKED_MISSING_MTP_COMPONENT`.

The specification already exists in [`docs/evaluation/SPECULATIVE_DECODING.md`](../evaluation/SPECULATIVE_DECODING.md):
five execution modes, the metrics (`TTFT`, `ITL`, throughput, acceptance rate, accepted tokens per verifier
step) and the constraint that governs the whole subject — *"QUALITY MUST NEVER BE HIDDEN BY SPEEDUP"*,
expressed as `max(speedup)` subject to `Δ(quality) ≥ −ε`. Study 004's contribution is to make `ε` a
pre-registered number and `Δ(quality)` a four-mode measurement, so that a decode change which preserves
`call_f1` while moving the model's *decisions* cannot pass as a speedup.
